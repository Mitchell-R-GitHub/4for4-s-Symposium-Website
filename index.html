Who We Are

Team 4for4's is a multidisciplinary capstone team from Camosun College's Information and Computer Systems (ICS) and Interactive Media Developer (IMD) programs. We combine expertise in software development, augmented reality, design, and communication to create meaningful digital experiences. Our capstone project, Monument Unmaking, is an augmented reality application that invites users to explore public art in Victoria through a new lens. By pointing a mobile device at selected installations, users can experience interactive AR overlays that offer alternative perspectives and encourage reflection on the history, meaning, and context behind these works.

Our Mission

We combine technology, storytelling, design, and interactive media to create engaging experiences that connect people with the spaces, histories, and stories around them.
